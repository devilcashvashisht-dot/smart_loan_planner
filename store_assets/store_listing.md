```text
Take complete control of your loans, mortgages, and repayments with Smart Loan & EMI Planner — the fast, offline, and privacy-first financial calculation utility.

Whether you are applying for a home mortgage, auto loan, personal credit, or business financing, Smart Loan & EMI Planner calculates your exact monthly payment, total interest payable, and detailed amortization schedule in seconds.

KEY FEATURES:

📊 ACCURATE EMI CALCULATOR
• Instant monthly installment (EMI) calculation.
• Dynamic visual breakdown: see exactly what percentage goes to Principal vs. Interest.
• Flexible tenure toggle: calculate by Months or Years.
• Supports all global currencies ($, €, £, ₹, ¥, ₱, and more).

📅 COMPLETE AMORTIZATION STATEMENT & PDF EXPORT
• Month-by-month repayment schedule tracking opening balance, EMI, principal portion, interest portion, and remaining balance.
• Export an official, professional PDF amortization statement to share, print, or review with your loan officer.

💰 PREPAYMENT & EARLY PAYOFF CALCULATOR
• Discover how much money and time you save by making extra monthly payments or one-time lump sum prepayments.
• Watch your loan tenure shrink by months or even years!
• Calculate total interest saved on your mortgage or car loan.

⚖️ SIDE-BY-SIDE LOAN COMPARISON
• Compare two loan offers from different lenders with varying interest rates and tenures.
• Instantly see which offer is cheaper and how much total money you save.

🔒 100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY-FIRST
• No sign-in or account creation required.
• No internet connection needed to calculate.
• None of your personal financial numbers are ever sent to remote servers. All calculations happen entirely on your device.

WHO IS THIS APP FOR?
• Home buyers planning mortgage schedules and down payments.
• Car buyers calculating auto loan financing costs.
• Students & graduates managing personal education debt.
• Financial advisors, real estate agents, and loan officers needing fast, reliable numbers on the go.

Disclaimer:
Smart Loan & EMI Planner is a standalone financial calculation utility designed for estimation and educational planning purposes. It does not provide loans or act as a lender.
```
