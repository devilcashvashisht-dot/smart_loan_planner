Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Smart Loan & EMI Planner mobile application (the "Application") developed as a free/ad-supported utility.

1. Information Collection and Use

Smart Loan & EMI Planner is designed with a strict privacy-first and offline-first philosophy:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal identification information (such as name, email, or phone number) to use the Application.
  • Offline Calculations: All loan calculations, financial inputs, interest rates, and amortization schedules are computed entirely on your local device. We do not transmit or store your financial figures on any external server.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for the purpose of serving advertisements:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain device data (such as advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data) to deliver non-personalized or personalized advertising in accordance with Google's policies.

For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

4. Security

We value your trust in using our Application. Because no personal data is transmitted over the internet to our servers, your financial figures remain strictly within your device's memory and local sandbox.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any changes are effective immediately upon posting to this URL.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us through your developer listing page on the Google Play Store.
